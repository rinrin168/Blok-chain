Verify a certificate

Look up a certificate by its ID or by scanning the QR code, and see what the record says.

  • No account needed
  • Reads the contract on Sepolia
  • Takes a few seconds

The ID is printed on the certificate and sent in the email with the PDF.

Where to find the ID
  1. Certificate ID. Printed in the row near the middle of the PDF. Copy it into the box above.
  2. QR code. Bottom right of the PDF. Press Scan QR Code and point the camera at it, or scan it with a phone to open this page with the ID filled in.
  3. Email. The message that came with the PDF also has a link that opens this page with the ID already entered.
What is checked
  1. The certificate exists in the organization's records.
  2. Its hash matches the hash stored in the smart contract.
  3. It has not expired and has not been revoked.
What the result means
Valid
The record matches and the certificate is in force.
Expired
The expiry date has passed.
Revoked
The issuing organization withdrew it.
Where the record lives

The hash is stored in a smart contract on the Ethereum Sepolia test network. Every result includes the transaction hash and a link to Etherscan.
